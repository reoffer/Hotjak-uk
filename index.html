How Portable Heater turns a freezing room into a warm cocoon in 60 seconds

You're probably wondering: "What's the difference from a normal portable heater?"

Excellent question.

Because on the surface, Portable Heater is small. Compact. Almost discreet.

But inside, it's a little marvel of engineering.

Here's how it works:

1. Instaheat™ technology at the heart of the system

At the centre of Portable Heater sits a revolutionary ceramic element, originally developed for electric vehicles.

Unlike ordinary metal heating elements, which cool down the instant the power is cut, Instaheat™ ceramic holds its heat 30% longer.

The result? The device doesn't need to keep switching itself back on to hold the temperature.

It heats once, and the heat stays.

Fewer heating cycles = far less electricity used.

2. An intelligent convection system

Once the ceramic element is up to temperature, an ultra-quiet fan draws the cold air in from the room, passes it through the hot ceramic, and pushes that warm air evenly across the whole room.

No "hot spot" next to the heater and "cold spot" everywhere else.

The warmth spreads everywhere, quickly and evenly.

3. An intelligent thermostat that wastes nothing

This is where Portable Heater gets really clever.

Most portable heaters work in the dumbest way possible: they blast at full power, then stop, then blast at full power again, and so on.

It's an energy massacre.

Portable Heater, on the other hand, constantly adjusts its output to hold exactly the temperature you've chosen.

No more. No less.
